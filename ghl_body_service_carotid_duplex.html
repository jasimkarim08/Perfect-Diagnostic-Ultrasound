Clinical Overview

Carotid duplex sonography combines high-resolution B-mode imaging with pulse-wave spectral Doppler and color Doppler to measure blood velocity and visualize the walls of the carotid arteries. It is the gold standard, non-invasive method for detecting carotid artery narrowing and preventing ischemic stroke.

What This Examination Evaluates

Common & Internal Carotid Arteries (CCA / ICA)

Quantifies peak systolic velocity (PSV), end-diastolic velocity (EDV), and the ICA/CCA velocity ratio to grade stenosis according to consensus criteria.

Atherosclerotic Plaque Characterization

Differentiates homogeneous, heterogeneous, calcified, soft, or ulcerated plaques along the carotid bulb and bifurcation.

External Carotid & Vertebral Arteries

Evaluates external carotid flow patterns and assesses vertebral artery flow direction to identify subclavian steal syndrome or vertebral hypoplasia.

Stroke & TIA Risk Assessment

Essential workup for patients with transient ischemic attacks (TIA), carotid bruits, amaurosis fugax, focal neurological deficits, syncope, or high cardiovascular risk.

Patient Preparation Guidelines

No special preparation or fasting is needed. Wear a loose, comfortable collarless shirt or blouse. Avoid wearing neck jewelry on the day of the examination.

Doctor Orders & Referrals

Detailed reports include grayscale plaque morphological descriptions, color Doppler documentation, and spectral velocity waveforms conforming to the Society of Radiologists in Ultrasound (SRU) consensus criteria.
