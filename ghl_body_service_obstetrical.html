Clinical Overview

Obstetrical ultrasound is a non-invasive, radiation-free diagnostic imaging modality used to monitor pregnancy, assess fetal growth and anatomical development, and ensure maternal health. Every examination at Perfect Diagnostic Ultrasound is performed using advanced digital sonography systems and interpreted by board-certified radiologists with rapid 24–48hr report delivery to your obstetrician or midwife.

What This Examination Evaluates

First Trimester Viability & Dating

Confirmation of intrauterine pregnancy, precise gestational age dating via crown-rump length (CRL), early fetal heartbeat detection, and exclusion of ectopic pregnancy.

Second Trimester Anatomy Survey (18–22 Weeks)

Detailed evaluation of fetal organs including cranial ventricles, facial profile, four-chamber cardiac views and outflow tracts, spine alignment, kidneys, stomach bubble, bladder, limbs, placental location, and amniotic fluid index.

Third Trimester Growth & Biophysical Profile (BPP)

Monitoring fetal growth parameters (BPD, HC, AC, FL), estimated fetal weight (EFW), fetal position/presentation, umbilical artery Doppler flow, and amniotic fluid volume.

Elective 3D/4D Fetal Sonography

Volumetric 3D surface rendering and real-time 4D motion capturing expressive fetal facial movements, yawns, and smiles in a warm, welcoming outpatient setting.

Patient Preparation Guidelines

For first-trimester examinations (< 14 weeks), please drink 32 oz of water finished 1 hour before your appointment and do not empty your bladder. For second- and third-trimester anatomy surveys, normal hydration is recommended, along with a light snack 30 minutes prior to encourage fetal activity.

Doctor Orders & Referrals

We accept obstetrical orders on any standard physician requisition pad or EHR printout. Routine and high-risk pregnancy evaluations receive prompt radiologist reports within 24–48 hours.
