How Outpatient Referrals Work

A frictionless coordination process designed to make ultrasound scheduling effortless for your medical staff and patients.

01

Order Generated

Your provider creates an ultrasound requisition on your standard clinic order pad, hospital EHR sheet, or prescription pad specifying clinical indication & ICD-10.

02

Order Transmitted

Fax the order to (708) 671-6711 or hand it to the patient. We accept orders for all abdominal, pelvic, OB, vascular, cardiac, and small parts services.

03

Patient Examination

The patient receives a dedicated, radiation-free ultrasound in our Chicago facility. Operating by appointment only ensures zero wait times and complete patient focus.

04

Radiologist Report

A board-certified radiologist evaluates and signs the diagnostic report, transmitted to your office within 24–48 hours. STAT critical findings are phoned immediately.
