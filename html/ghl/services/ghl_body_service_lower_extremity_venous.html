Clinical Overview

Deep vein thrombosis (DVT) is a potentially life-threatening condition where a blood clot forms in the deep veins of the legs, posing a risk of pulmonary embolism (PE). Our clinic provides prompt, appointment-based venous duplex evaluations performed with compression sonography and spectral Doppler flow analysis.

What This Examination Evaluates

Deep Vein Compression Testing

Full coaptation testing of the common femoral vein (CFV), femoral vein (FV), deep femoral vein (profunda), and popliteal vein to confirm complete lumen collapse.

Calf Vein Assessment

Systematic evaluation of posterior tibial, peroneal, and gastrocnemius veins for isolated calf vein thrombosis.

Spectral Doppler Hemodynamics

Assesses spontaneous flow, respiratory phasicity, and response to distal augmentation maneuvers to rule out proximal pelvic vein obstruction.

Superficial Venous Thrombophlebitis

Examines the great saphenous vein (GSV) and small saphenous vein (SSV) for superficial clot, wall thickening, and saphenofemoral junction competence.

Patient Preparation Guidelines

No fasting or special preparation is necessary. Wear loose-fitting trousers or shorts that can be easily rolled up or removed. Continue all medications, including anticoagulants.

Doctor Orders & Referrals

Positive DVT findings are treated with urgent physician notification. A board-certified radiologist report is faxed immediately to the referring provider's office.
