Clinical Overview

Renal and bladder ultrasound is a non-invasive, radiation-free diagnostic test assessing the upper and lower urinary tracts. It is the initial imaging study of choice for evaluating flank pain, suspected kidney stones, hematuria (blood in urine), recurrent urinary tract infections, and renal impairment.

What This Examination Evaluates

Renal Dimensions & Cortical Thickness

Measures bipolar renal length and assesses parenchymal cortical thickness and corticomedullary differentiation to evaluate chronic kidney disease (CKD) or atrophy.

Nephrolithiasis (Kidney Stones)

Identifies echogenic calculi, measures stone size, evaluates posterior acoustic shadowing, and checks for twinkling artifacts with color Doppler.

Hydronephrosis & Obstruction

Grades caliectasis and pelviectasis (mild, moderate, severe) to identify urinary tract obstruction caused by ureteral calculi, strictures, or external compression.

Urinary Bladder & Post-Void Residual (PVR)

Examines bladder lumen, wall thickness, diverticula, and calculates precise pre-void and post-void residual urine volumes to assess urinary retention.

Patient Preparation Guidelines

Drink 24–32 oz of water finished 1 hour before your appointment so that your urinary bladder is comfortably distended. Do not urinate until the sonographer completes the pre-void bladder measurements. You will then be given time to void for the post-void evaluation.

Doctor Orders & Referrals

Evaluated by board-certified radiologists with detailed measurements of both kidneys, cortical thickness, hydronephrosis grading, and pre/post-void bladder volumes.
