Thyroid & Small Parts Diagnostic Ultrasound

High-frequency superficial ultrasound providing detailed evaluation of thyroid nodules, goiter, cervical lymph nodes, and scrotal/testicular structures.

Schedule Scan Online Call (872) 227-2321

Clinical Overview

High-frequency linear ultrasound probes deliver extraordinary spatial resolution for superficial soft-tissue structures located close to the skin surface. Our clinic specializes in detailed sonography of the thyroid gland, neck lymph nodes, and scrotal structures evaluated by board-certified radiologists.

What This Examination Evaluates

Thyroid Gland Architecture & Volume

Accurate 3D volume measurements of right and left thyroid lobes and isthmus, assessing parenchymal echotexture for thyroiditis (e.g. Hashimoto's, Graves' disease).

Thyroid Nodules & ACR TI-RADS Scoring

Detailed sonographic characterization of nodule size, composition, echogenicity, margins, shape, and microcalcifications with TI-RADS radiologist risk stratification.

Cervical Lymph Node Chains

Evaluation of anterior and lateral cervical lymph nodes to differentiate benign reactive lymphadenopathy from suspicious nodes.

Scrotal & Testicular Sonography

High-frequency imaging assessing testicular pain, swelling, hydrocele, varicocele, epididymitis, orchitis, and testicular microcalcifications or masses.

Patient Preparation Guidelines

For thyroid imaging: Wear a comfortable, loose open-collar shirt or blouse; avoid turtlenecks. Remove necklaces before the scan. For scrotal imaging: No special prep is needed; wear loose, comfortable clothing.

Doctor Orders & Referrals

All thyroid nodule reports feature formal ACR TI-RADS scoring with precise millimeter measurements and vascular color Doppler documentation to guide follow-up or biopsy decisions.
